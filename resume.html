Marketing Framework

With over 10 years building and shipping marketing websites, I've come to see that all great companies and brands live at the intersection of entertainment, value, and consistency. I design with these four core pillars to get there:

1. Website core vitals

Speed, accessibility, responsiveness, SEO, and analytics. Faster, more accessible sites rank higher, reach more users, and convert better — and you can't improve what you don't measure.

2. Architecture

A modern, well-maintained tech stack with minimal legacy code. Less time fighting tech debt means more time shipping campaigns, optimizing the experience, and earning user trust.

3. Growth

Continuous A/B experimentation paired with shipping net-new features. Test hypotheses, keep what works, and grow intentionally — more growth means more responsibility, so scale on purpose.

4. Value driven content

Give value freely — useful playbooks, tools, and education — before asking for a signup. Trust compounds, organic traffic follows, and the platform earns the right to ask.
